# Test-day checklist

## The day before

- Read your invitation again: which tests, the deadline, and anything it says about equipment.
- Decide when you will take each test. Aon says you can do them whenever you like and do not need to take them all in one go. Pick times when you are alert, not straight after work or sport.
- If you need an adjustment, such as extra time, contact the employer now. Aon says adjustments are decided case by case.
- Plan to use a computer, not a phone. Aon says most tests run on phones and tablets but strongly recommends a PC or laptop.

## One hour before

- Update your browser and check your internet connection.
- Put out a calculator, a pen and paper. Aon recommends all three for scales numerical.
- Turn off your phone and tell anyone at home you cannot be interrupted. A started test cannot be paused.
- Close other tabs and apps.

## In the first minute

- **Use the untimed examples.** Aon lets you repeat them as often as you like. Do them until the controls feel natural.
- Read the summary page before you start. It repeats the key rules.
- For numerical and verbal: once the clock starts, spend about 30 seconds reading the tab titles and units before the first statement.

## During the test

- Answer what you can justify. Wrong answers usually cost points.
- On numerical and verbal: if the screen does not settle it, CANNOT SAY.
- On ix: name the rule, check all nine, then click.
- Keep the same care in the last minute as in the first.
- If a test freezes or your browser crashes, do not panic. Aon says to contact the company that invited you so they can unblock it.

## Afterwards

- Take a break before the next test.
- Write down which items felt hard, while you remember. It helps if there is a retest at interview.
- Ask the employer if a feedback report is available. Aon says each test has one; the employer decides whether to share it.

## Last practice before the real one

If you have time, do one short timed run the day before, not the hour before. The [free Aon cut-e style practice test on PrepClubs](https://prepclubs.com/free-practice-tests/aon-cut-e?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=study-notes&utm_content=aon-cut-e) covers all three formats.
