# Other Aon tests you may be sent

Your invitation may include tests other than scales numerical, verbal and ix, or instead of them. Aon's candidate page lists these, with the lengths below. We have not written full guides for them, but knowing the format in advance removes most of the surprise.

## Aptitude and skills tests

| Aon's name | Length (Aon) | What it involves |
|---|---|---|
| Language Skills (scales lt) | 10 minutes | Three task types: fill in blanks, choose words from definitions, correct spelling errors |
| Mechanical Reasoning (scales mtu) | 15 minutes, 24 tasks | Mechanical and technical understanding from diagrams |
| Understanding of Basic Instructions (scales verbal instruct) | 8 minutes, 24 tasks | Judge statements against written instructions |
| Basic Numerical Comprehension (scales eql) | 5 minutes | Basic arithmetic by mental calculation |
| Numeracy (digitChallenge) | 6 minutes | Basic arithmetic by mental calculation |
| Working Memory (gridChallenge) | 9 minutes, nine tasks | Hold something in mind while processing other information |
| Complex Planning Capability (motionChallenge) | 6 minutes | Plan moves to get from a start state to a goal state |
| Ability to Concentrate (scales e3+) | 2 minutes | Concentrate over time and react quickly to signals |

## Logic tests

| Aon's name | Length (Aon) | What it involves |
|---|---|---|
| Inductive Logical Reasoning (scales clx) | 6 minutes | Find common patterns and relationships in a group of items |
| Inductive-logical Thinking (scales cls) | 12 minutes, 12 tasks | Work out the rules in given patterns and apply them |
| Deductive-logical Thinking (gapChallenge) | 6 minutes | Use the logic of a grid to fill the empty cell |
| Deductive Reasoning (switchChallenge) | 6 minutes | Find the 4-digit sequence that gives the required result |

## Situational judgement

| Aon's name | Length (Aon) | What it involves |
|---|---|---|
| Gamified Situational Judgment Test (chatAssess) | 20 minutes | Reply to work messages, measuring judgement in work scenarios |

Source for all tables: [Aon, Prepare for your Online Assessment](https://www.aon.com/en/capabilities/talent-and-rewards/prepare-for-your-online-assessment), checked 24 November 2026. Where a row has no task count, Aon does not give one.

## What carries over from these notes

- **scales verbal instruct** uses the same idea as scales verbal: judge a statement against text. The [verbal guide](sections/scales-verbal.md) method applies.
- **scales clx and scales cls** are inductive, like scales ix: find the rule first, then apply it. The [ix guide](sections/scales-ix.md) habit of naming the rule family helps.
- **scales eql and digitChallenge** are mental arithmetic. The estimation habits in the [numerical guide](sections/scales-numerical.md) help, but practise times tables and percentages too.
- For every Aon test, the [scoring rules](scoring.md) and the session structure in the [test overview](test-overview.md) are the same.
